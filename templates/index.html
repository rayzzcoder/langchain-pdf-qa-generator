How it works

1

Upload

Pick a PDF of your study material. Your file stays on the server running this app.

2

Generate

The app reads your document, writes 10 questions about its key ideas, then finds the answers inside the text.

3

Study

Read the questions here, or download a CSV that opens in Excel and Google Sheets.

Good to know

  • Generating takes a few minutes. Keep this tab open until it finishes.
  • Answers come only from your document. AI can still make mistakes, so check important points against the source.
  • Each new upload replaces the previous results, so download the CSV if you want to keep it.
Built with FastAPI, LangChain, Groq and Gemini embeddings (FAISS search)
